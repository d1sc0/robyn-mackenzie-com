There is a direct connection between the tactile rhythm of carving lino blocks and the ambient soundscapes playing in the background. When working through repetitive manual tasks or wrestling with dense typography hierarchies, music acts as a focusing scaffold.

### What's in Heavy Rotation

* **Nils Frahm — *All Melody*:** The perfect balance of acoustic piano mechanics and analog modular synthesizers.
* **Floating Points — *Promises* (with Pharoah Sanders):** A masterclass in patient spatial storytelling.
* **Four Tet — *New Energy*:** Subtle percussive textures that keep pace without demanding verbal attention.

> "A good studio playlist shouldn't compete with your thoughts — it should widen the room they have to breathe in."
