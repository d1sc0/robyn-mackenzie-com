When I’m not designing, you might find me rollerskating, volunteering at parkrun, or cooking some very nearly underbaked cookies. I’m Dorset born and bred, but my work — and curiosity — reach far beyond.

Alongside finishing my BA Graphic Design degree at **Arts University Bournemouth (AUB)**, I work as a freelance content designer — helping organisations make information more accessible, usable, and human.

I’ve previously worked within the **NHS** on digital transformation projects, supporting everything from user testing and qualitative interviews to training material design.

Now, I’m looking for opportunities that stretch across public, civic, and cultural sectors — projects that centre care, drive progressive change, and leave things better than they were :)
