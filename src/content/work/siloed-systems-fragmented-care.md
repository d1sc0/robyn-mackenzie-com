### **Bridging the Maternity Care Divide**

Focusing on maternity services across the pan-Dorset healthcare system, this project investigates how disparate records impact both patient agency and clinical workflows.

> "How might service design transform fragmented clinical records into an empowering, continuous care experience for families?"

**Participatory Research & Systems Mapping**

To uncover the lived reality behind systemic data silos, the project combined qualitative user interviews with a collaborative workshop involving both NHS staff and service users. Mapping the tension points between digital platforms (such as BadgerNotes) and traditional paper notes revealed where technical architecture often fails human needs.

- **Touchpoint 1:** Comparative Patient Journey Maps (Digital vs. Paper Records)
- **Touchpoint 2:** Multi-Stakeholder Workshop Syntheses & Service Blueprints
- **Touchpoint 3:** Strategic Framework for Digital Inclusion & Cultural Adoption

The findings demonstrate that while digital tools offer clear paths toward empowerment, real systemic change requires pairing service design with institutional culture shifts and proactive digital inclusion.
