### 200 Years, 200 Stories, 200 Stations

The UK railway is more than tracks, signals, and timetable timetables. It is the backdrop to tearful farewells, daily meditative commutes, reunion hugs on cold platforms, and late-night adventures.

### The Service Framework

```
Physical Prompt (Postcards at Platforms)
       │
       ▼
Passenger Reflections (Handwritten Memories)
       │
       ▼
Digital Station Archive (Public Living Tapestry)
```

By placing physical submission dropboxes alongside a mobile-first scanning kiosk at regional stations across Britain, travellers of all generations were invited to contribute to an open living archive.

> "A national celebration built not from corporate statistics, but from human voices and everyday journeys."
