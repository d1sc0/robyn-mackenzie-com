### Measuring What Really Matters

**Tally** is a speculative, self-initiated project exploring alternative ways to evaluate national progress. Rather than focusing solely on gross domestic product (GDP), the project proposes six human-centred pillars:

1. **Food Security & Nourishment**
2. **Community Wellbeing & Connection**
3. **Ecological Resilience**
4. **Accessible Public Transport**
5. **Decent, Secure Housing**
6. **Lifelong Creative Education**

> "What would happen if we measured what matters to people, rather than abstract fiscal spreadsheets?"

### Visual Language & Lino Printing

To give the campaign a tangible, grounded warmth, each icon category was hand-carved in linoleum and hand-printed before digital vectorization. This human imperfection contrasts with the sterile aesthetics often associated with government white papers and fiscal reports.

```markdown
* Touchpoint 1: The "People's Budget" Summary Sheet
* Touchpoint 2: Street-level voting prompts and ballot sticker packs
* Touchpoint 3: Interactive civic telemetry dashboard
```

The outcome demonstrates how speculative design can be applied to complex civic systems to spark meaningful public dialogue.
