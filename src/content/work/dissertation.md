### From Transaction to Relationship

Traditional digital service transformation in the public sector has frequently prioritized algorithmic throughput and cost reduction. But what happens to the vulnerable service user when empathy is designed out of the loop?

This dissertation investigates:

1. **Relational vs Transactional Touchpoints:** Why interpersonal continuity matters in healthcare and social care delivery.
2. **Accessible Content Strategy:** Writing for crisis states and low cognitive bandwidth situations.
3. **Equitable Metrics:** Measuring dignity, trust, and agency alongside response times.

> "A service that is 100% automated and 0% understanding is not an advancement — it is an evasion of responsibility."
