### Opening the Concert Hall

**Resonance** explores how symphonic music institutions can break through perceptual barriers of elitism and formality.

Working from user interviews with university students and young creatives across London, this project established a new typographic voice, interactive outdoor listening stations, and accessible program guides.

### Inclusive Spatial Touchpoints

* **Acoustic Wayfinding:** Signage informed by ambient acoustics to guide visitors gently through historic venues.
* **Low-Cost Community Passes:** Transparent digital booking system integrated directly into regional arts networks.
